Entry for the [One-Liner Laugh Lab](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae)
bounty on make.fun: one original funny line, at most 200 characters, answering
the prompt — the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock — written to
`/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

The line:

> The dryer took the left one — I only finished the job.

It was picked from six candidates scored against the judge brief's criteria
in order: it answers the prompt directly, lands a deadpan twist (the dryer
already broke the pair), uses a fresh angle no sampled field entry used (no
legal defence, no "I adopted it", no job-description bureaucrat), fits the
situation exactly, and stays tight at 54 characters with an em-dash payoff.
